Privacy Policy

We may share limited data with trusted service providers such as payment gateways, analytics platforms, email delivery tools, and communication tools solely for service delivery and business operations.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for service delivery, legal compliance, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, and record-keeping.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your personal information. While no system is fully risk-free, we continuously review and improve security controls.

8. Your Rights

  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion where legally permissible

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for adult learners and working professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children without appropriate consent.
