Shocking Facts About Data Engineering Most People Don't Know...

You don’t need a fancy degree for some high-paying data engineering jobs

Around a quarter of data engineer job listings today don’t even specify a university degree, meaning people with the right skills (even without a traditional degree) can still land high-paying roles — as long as they can prove what they can do.

There’s a SHORTAGE of qualified data engineers — even though demand is exploding

Companies all over the world are struggling to find enough people who can build and manage the complex data systems that power AI and business tools. There simply aren’t enough trained data engineers for how many jobs there are. This is one reason salaries keep going up in many markets — companies are competing for the few experts available.

Even with high demand, many data engineering jobs have huge applicant crowds

Some online job postings for data engineer roles show 1,000+ people applying within just 1–2 days after the job is listed — meaning competition is extremely fierce and having the right resume or network can make or break your chances.
