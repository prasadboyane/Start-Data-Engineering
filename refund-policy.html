Refund Policy

Effective Date: 22 Feb 2026

At Ekah Solutions, we aim to provide high-quality digital learning products and clear support. This policy explains when refunds are available and how requests are handled.

1. General Policy

Refunds are not applicable for products/services unless explicitly stated at the time of purchase, on the checkout page, or in the offer details.

2. Refund Eligibility (Where Applicable)

  • Duplicate payment for the same product/order
  • Successful payment but no course/material access delivered
  • Transaction failure where amount is debited and order is not created
  • Specific offer-based guarantees (if clearly mentioned in campaign terms)

3. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Change of mind after access is delivered
  • Failure to review product details before purchase
  • Delays caused by incorrect email/phone submitted by the user
  • Any case outside explicit guarantee terms

4. How to Request a Refund

Send your request with the following details to our support team:

  • Registered name, email, and phone number
  • Order/payment ID and transaction screenshot
  • Reason for refund request and any relevant proof

5. Review and Processing Timeline

Refund requests are reviewed in 3-5 business days. Once approved, refunds are processed within 7-10 business days to the original payment method.

6. Chargebacks and Disputes

If you initiate a chargeback without contacting support first, access to purchased content may be paused while the dispute is reviewed.
